Each of the 72 references in this Supplement has a finite family of claim nodes, a finite family of time contents, a finite family of transfer events, and a finite family of results. For each such reference, the inclusion

𝖨𝗇𝗂𝗍Raudit,phys⊆𝖤𝗇𝗏Raudit,evid⊆𝖭𝗈𝖽𝖾Raudit,claim\mathsf{Init}^{\mathrm{audit\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak phys}}_{R}\subseteq% \mathsf{Env}^{\mathrm{audit\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak evid}}_{R}\subseteq% \mathsf{Node}^{\mathrm{audit\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak claim}}_{R}

holds. By Proposition 7.107 , the transfer and result tables for each reference determine a unique audit-boundary directed hypergraph, together with its reachability, primitive time roots, fourfold result partition, and minimal primitive-time-root support family. For a reference that directly supplies typed finite audit input, Proposition 7.106 determines the same audit boundary as its canonical realization.
