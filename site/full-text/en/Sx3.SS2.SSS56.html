Placement of grounding obligations

Placement of grounding obligations

The three time contents audited in this source are accepted as already formed temporal inputs rather than generated from nontemporal physical inputs before use. The placement determination for their physical-time grounding obligations is backgrounded by primitive-time input. This determination follows by comparing the use of each time content in a law operation or result derivation with the absence, before that use, of its corresponding generation or formation condition as an input, formation condition, evaluation condition, or failure condition.

Evidence of actual failure

Evidence of actual failure

The complete determination and residual-root determination concern physical-time admissibility and are distinct from the actual failure of a concrete condition governing a result value or applicability domain. Neither the original-source formal evidence nor the LCTR audit evidence for this source establishes such an actual failure of a target condition. The actual-failure determination is indeterminate from the original-source evidence, and actual failure is not inferred from the backgrounding of a grounding obligation alone.

Downstream reach

Downstream reach

The extent of reach to primitive-time-root-dependent results is determined source by source by the primitive-time-root–result dependence relation and minimal primitive-time-root support reconstructed in this division. This is structural reach. Because no actual failure of a concrete condition has been established, the determination of actual reach from a failure location to a result is indeterminate from the original-source evidence. Structural reach does not substitute for actual reach.

Repair scope

Repair scope

On the basis of structural reach, the repair targets are the corresponding time contents and the generation or formation conditions required before their use. The recalculation scope is limited to results reached from a failure location when actual failure has independently been established. Because actual failure is indeterminate in this source, this repair and recalculation specification is conditional. If that condition is satisfied, the target results are regenerated or recalculated from the repaired inputs, or their validity domains are restricted. Post-recalculation values are fixed by the audit result.

Proposition S.564 (Complete audit of time use in Van Raamsdonk 2010).

The source has 37 semantic components, 3 time contents, 0 time-content transfers, and 10 results. Both the complete-generation and local-reach counts for time contents are zero; Results 1–8 depend on primitive-time roots.

No edge outputs a time content, so adding the source formal-evidence environment to the nontemporal initial set reaches no time content. Results 1–8 have nonempty time premises; Results 9 and 10 have empty time premises. ∎

All three time contents are primitive-time roots. Results 1–8 depend on at least one root; Results 9 and 10 have empty primitive-time-root support.

Proof.

With reachability conditioned on the evidence environment of Definition S.561, Results 9 and 10 are reachable without primitive-time roots; adding all three roots makes all ten results reachable. ∎

Proposition S.566 (Minimal primitive-time-root support in the Van Raamsdonk 2010 source).

Individual audits

Quantum entanglement and classical spacetime connectivity

The first statement argues that quantum entanglement among the degrees of freedom in a nonperturbative description of quantum gravity is intimately related to the emergence of classically connected spacetime. The statement asserts a structural correspondence, within AdS/CFT, between quantum entanglement in the boundary state and the spatial connectivity of the corresponding classical bulk. Its independent grounds are the state–geometry comparisons in the original source that relate the product state of two noninteracting CFTs to a disconnected bulk, the TFD state to a connected eternal AdS black hole, and quantum entanglement to classical spatial connectivity. The evidential force belongs to the AdS/CFT correspondence, boundary states, bulk geometry, and the connectivity analyzed by the original source. In the two-CFT example, the correspondence between entanglement and classical connectivity is conditional on the identification of the TFD state with the eternal AdS black hole. The corresponding formal evidence comprises Physical/Formal Claims 8–13 and 25 in Definition S.553, and Results 1–3, 8, and 10 in Definition S.560.

Disentanglement and separation of regions

The second statement relates, within a state family in which the entanglement of degrees of freedom associated with two regions decreases, the geometric change by which the regions pull apart and pinch off to standard measures of entanglement. The statement relates a change in the bulk minimal area, correlation distance, and spatial connectivity to a family of states in which entanglement between boundary subsystems is varied. Its independent grounds are the three relations connecting reduced-state entanglement entropy to RT minimal area, mutual information to connected correlations, and heavy-operator two-point correlations to bulk geodesic distance. At fixed positive Newton constant, decreasing entropy implies decreasing minimal area. Divergent distance corresponds to the zero-mutual-information limit under the correlator identification and approximation conditions in Definition S.559. The evidential force belongs to the holographic states, region partition, entanglement deformation, and bulk connectivity constructed by the original source. The deformation compares states with different entanglement. Figure 4 holds boundary geometry fixed, and Figure 5 compares spatial sections of eternal black holes at different inverse temperatures. The corresponding formal evidence comprises Physical/Formal Claims 14–24 in Definition S.553, and Results 4–9 in Definition S.560.

Evidence for the correspondence and incomplete understanding of the emergence mechanism

The third statement jointly states that extensive evidence supports the validity of the gauge-theory/gravity correspondence and that a deep understanding of why and how spacetime or gravity emerges from field-theory degrees of freedom has not been attained. The statement distinguishes particular agreements that support the correspondence from theoretical understanding that explains the mechanism of spacetime and gravity emergence, and places the latter among unresolved research problems. Its independent grounds are that formal results support particular correspondences among states, geometry, and correlations, while the original source itself distinguishes them from a complete explanation of the emergence mechanism and identifies the investigation of spacetime emergence from quantum-information structure as a research program. The evidential force belongs to the existing agreements supporting the correspondence and the state of explanatory attainment at the time of publication. The supporting evidence is the statement of incomplete understanding in the introduction, Physical/Formal Claims 8 and 25 in Definition S.553, and Result 10 in Definition S.560.
