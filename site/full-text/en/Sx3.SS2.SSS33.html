Definition S.299 (Formal objects and explicit assumptions in Barbour 1994 I).

Barbour takes complete relative configurations of the universe as fundamental and discusses the operational recovery of time along classical histories. For particle systems he uses the fixed-energy Jacobi principle and best matching; for geometrodynamics he uses matching between three-geometries, local ephemeris time and the BSW action. His discussions of subsystem clocks, astronomical ephemeris time, atomic time and proper-time clocks involve their respective dynamical conditions and comparisons of clock rates.

The Jacobi construction uses a conservative particle system, fixed energy, a configuration metric and a potential. Its Newtonian correspondence through best matching uses zero total angular momentum of the universe; agreement of subsystem-clock rates uses effective isolation and energy-conserving parameters. Four-dimensional reconstruction in geometrodynamics is conditional on a solution of the variational problem determining equilocality. Specialization to general relativity uses the DeWitt supermetric and three-dimensional scalar curvature.

Evidence Hierarchy

Barbour discusses variational constructions in classical mechanics and geometrodynamics, existing timekeeping practices and theoretical claims about quantization. The source reports zero directly measured objects and zero direct-measurement predicates from measurements of its own. Solar-system observations, atomic-clock networks and proper-time clocks are examples of time determination discussed by Barbour.

Generation Judgment

The complete LCTR generation count and source-evidence-conditioned local reach count are both zero, leaving 18 primitive-time roots. Barbour’s ephemeris-time construction determines duration along realized histories of configurations or geometries. The source’s timekeeping practices and comparison theories use clock processes, temporal evolution of the environment or external time.

Placement of the Grounding Obligation

Ephemeris-time transfers presuppose succession along a realized history; extraction of atomic time presupposes temporal evolution in the clock network and environmental model; the proper-time correspondence presupposes local ephemeris time along geometric evolution. The durations and times output by these transfers depend on those premises. None of the 18 time contents belongs to the generation closure from nontemporal physical inputs or the local closure with source formal evidence. The LCTR placement judgment is “backgrounded by primitive-time inputs.”

Individual Statements

Determination of Time by All Variables

Original text:

Time is not any one variable; it is created by all variables working in concert.

The statement occurs on p. 2860, §7. Barbour criticizes choosing an arbitrary variable, such as the longitude of Mars, as a clock. He discusses a time parameter for which effectively noninteracting subsystem clocks keep in step up to constant proportionality. That parameter is determined from changes in all variables so that energy is conserved in each subsystem.

“Time as Such” and Instants as Relative Configurations

Original text:

It is argued that time as such does not exist but that instants, defined as complete relative configurations of the universe, do.

The statement occurs in the abstract on p. 2853. Barbour presents a timeless kinematics grounded in complete relative configurations of the universe. The same abstract describes the operational recovery of time within classical histories and connects this construction to the interpretation of a static wavefunction in quantum gravity.

Individual Audit

Determination of Time by All Variables

In the first statement, Barbour describes a construction that obtains a parameter from changes in all variables, subject to energy conservation and agreement of subsystem-clock rates. Choosing an arbitrary single variable of each subsystem as a clock fails to give the agreement of rates he requires. The source’s integral uses a realized configurational history and its direction to determine ephemeris-time differences; subsystem comparisons use effective isolation and constant proportionality. The corresponding formal evidence comprises Claims 9–14 and 20, Transfers 1 and 3–4, and Results 4–5 and 8.

“Time as Such” and Instants as Relative Configurations

The second statement expresses Barbour’s timeless kinematics grounded in complete relative configurations of the universe. His classical construction recovers ephemeris time along realized histories of relative configurations and local proper time in the specialization to general relativity. The physical succession, clock rates and proper-time correspondence used by this construction are temporal inputs to this audit. Barbour defers recovery of experienced time from the static quantum description to the companion paper. The corresponding formal evidence comprises Claims 1, 4, 7, 21, 28–31 and 36, Transfers 1–2 and 7–8, and Results 4, 11, 13, 17–18, 20 and 24.
