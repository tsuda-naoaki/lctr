Individual Statements

Conjecture of Duality

Original text:

We conjecture that compactifications of M/string theory on various Anti-deSitter spacetimes is dual to various conformal field theories.

Source location: Abstract on the unnumbered opening page. Following the argument concerning the supergravity Hilbert sector, Maldacena conjectures a duality of full M/string theory and CFT.

Need for AdS Boundary Conditions

Original text:

A theory in AdS spacetime is not completely well defined since there is a horizon and it is also necessary to give some boundary conditions at infinity.

Source location: Section 1, p. 1. Maldacena identifies the horizon and asymptotic boundary in the AdS description used and requires boundary conditions to define the theory.

Duality Conjecture with Unspecified Boundary Conditions

Original text:

We will conjecture that the full quantum M/string-theory on AdS space, plus suitable boundary conditions is dual to the corresponding brane theory. We are not going to specify the boundary conditions in AdS, we leave this interesting problem for the future.

Source location: Section 1, p. 1. The correspondence of full quantum M/string theory with the brane theory requires suitable boundary conditions, whose specification Maldacena leaves to future work.

Local Proper Energy

The fourth statement shows Maldacena’s use of local energy conjugate to proper time. Worldvolume time, fixed energy, proper time and proper energy are Time Contents 2–5. The fifth statement is Maldacena’s assessment of the proposal presented in that paper. Claims 36 and 44 and Results 29 and 37 record the proposal and its open tasks.
