Assumptions

Anderson examines facets of the Problem of Time in quantum gravity and several strategies. The ADM decomposition uses a specified spacelike foliation, and the JBB construction uses a real nonsingular integration domain. An internal-clock function, its local validity as a clock, and the solution for reduced evolution each have their corresponding conditions. The WKB construction uses a selected phase flow and approximation order. Conditional probabilities, records, histories and nonideal-clock evolution have their respective states, projectors, carriers and temporal variables.

Nontemporal Physical Inputs

The nontemporal configuration data, metric and energy, wave function, clock and record carriers, projectors, state and Hamiltonian, spatial geometry and canonical variables in Claims 1–7 form the generation-initial set. Temporal order, evolution in time and the clock constructions belong to the corresponding time contents and formal claims.
