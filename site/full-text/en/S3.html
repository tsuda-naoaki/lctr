3.2 Object-Specification Positions and Role-Type Positions

The base role-type set 𝖱𝗈𝗅\mathsf{Rol} of Definition 2.5 supplies the four component positions of a role-instance bundle. The pairing of an object-specification-index position from Definition 2.9 with a role-type position determines the object-specification membership and role-type membership of each role instance.

3.3 Role-Instance Identity and Implementation-Side Identity

By Definition 2.9, the identity of an object-specified role instance is determined by its object-specification index. The implementation-side identity of a 𝖱𝗈𝗅rlz\mathsf{Rol}^{\mathrm{rlz}}-derived component is determined by the value of the corresponding realization map in Definition 3.2. The identity of the abstraction source of a 𝔅\mathfrak{B}-derived component is determined by the physical object being tracked that its object-specification index specifies.

For a construction involving multiple object specifications, physical carriers, source sequences, or observer-side reception phases, an indexed family is used that retains the object-specification, role-type, sequence, and reception-phase positions.

3.4 Multiplicity and Physical-Placement Conditions

The role-typed physical-carrier correspondence pattern of Definition 3.4 specifies three component conditions for two distinct object-specification indices: the ℭ\mathfrak{C}-shared or ℭ\mathfrak{C}-separated type, the 𝔇\mathfrak{D}-shared or 𝔇\mathfrak{D}-separated type, and the 𝔒\mathfrak{O}-shared or 𝔒\mathfrak{O}-distributed type. The 𝔅\mathfrak{B}-derived components are retained as the family of abstraction-source correspondences equipped with object-specification positions in Definition 3.2.

For a construction with multiple clock-side, record-side, or object-state-side sequences, or multiple observer-side reception phases, the sequence and object-specification positions are retained, and the realization specifications and reception conditions are distinguished for each role type.
