Audit Conclusions

Proposition S.702 (Complete Audit of Time Use in the Heisenberg Source).

Complete generation and local reach both yield zero cases, and all fifteen time contents remain primitive time roots. Physical Result 1 depends on Time Content 15.

Proof.

There are no transfer edges that output time contents. The sole result edge takes Time Content 15 and Source Claims 1–5 as inputs. Thus neither the physical initial set nor the source evidence environment reaches a time content, and reaching Result 1 requires the addition of Time Content 15. ∎

Statement Evidence 1: Nonexistence of Time before the Universe

Original quotation

Time has been created together with the world, it belongs to the world, therefore time did not exist before the universe existed.

Primary-source location

Chapter VII, printed p. 125, PDF p. 144.

Minimal context

The paragraph in which Heisenberg presents Augustine’s discussion of the creation of the universe and time in the Confessions.

Attribution

Werner Heisenberg presenting Augustine’s argument.

Statement Evidence 2: Temporal Presupposition in the Vocabulary of Creation

Original quotation

Therefore, it is impossible to define in rational terms what could be meant by the phrase “time has been created.”

Primary-source location

Chapter VII, printed p. 125, PDF p. 144.

Minimal context

The conclusion immediately following the observation that created means that something which did not exist before came into being, and that this meaning presupposes a concept of time.

Attribution

Werner Heisenberg’s own conceptual analysis.

Common Evidence with a Specified Domain of Application

Individual Audit

Nonexistence of Time before the Universe

Interpretive possibility

The original text presents Augustine’s argument that time belongs to the world and therefore did not exist before its creation. The absence of a defined time coordinate in a cosmological model and the nonexistence of time itself are distinct propositions.

Independent grounds

Heisenberg’s explicit presentation of Augustine’s argument.

Boundary of evidential force

The original text supports that Heisenberg presented the argument.

Corresponding Time Contents and Statement Dependencies

Time Contents 11 and 2 and Relation 2 in Table 669. Here Time Contents 11 and 2 correspond to the presentation of Augustine’s argument. Physical Result 1 concerns general-relativistic cosmological models.

Temporal Presupposition in the Vocabulary of Creation

Interpretive possibility

This identifies a self-dependency in which the word asserting the creation of time already requires a temporal relation that represents before and after generation.

Independent grounds

The immediately preceding sentence that explicitly gives the ordinary meaning of created as the coming into being of something that did not exist before.

Boundary of evidential force

The original text identifies dependence on a concept of time through the before–after relation contained in the ordinary meaning of created.

Corresponding Time Contents and Statement Dependencies

Time Contents 11, 13, and 14 and Relation 3 in Table 669.
