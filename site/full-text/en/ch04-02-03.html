Proposition 4.146 (Structural distinction among exact operational time structures, comparison-order atlases, global order-coordinate quotients, and representational coordinates).

Assume 𝖮𝗉𝖾𝗋𝖺𝗍𝗂𝗏𝖾⁢(𝒳)\mathsf{Operative}(\mathcal{X}). For each k∈{1,2,3}k\in\{1\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak 2\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak 3\}, under 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋≤k⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{Repr}_{\leq k}[\mathsf{I}], 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤k⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq k}[\mathsf{I}] is generated in dependency order.

The exact operational time structure is a component of 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=1⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=1}[\mathsf{I}]; the comparison-order atlas is a component of 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=2⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=2}[\mathsf{I}]; and the global order-coordinate quotient on ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} and the canonical global order-coordinate projection are components of 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=3}[\mathsf{I}].

For each R∈𝖱𝗈𝗅cmpR\in\mathsf{Rol}^{\mathrm{cmp}}, once the selected representational codomain derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} and the order embedding into the representational codomain derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} in Definition 4.140 are given, the composition formula of Definition 4.140 determines the comparison representational coordinate derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}. Thus the exact operational time structure, comparison-order atlas, global order-coordinate quotient, and representational coordinate have distinct types and generation formulas.

Proof.

For k=1k=1, Definition 4.101, Lemma 4.102, Definition 4.103, Definition 4.104, Definition 4.110, Definition 4.111, and Definition 4.130 generate 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=1⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=1}[\mathsf{I}] in dependency order.

For k=2k=2, Proposition 4.145, Lemma 4.114, Definition 4.116, Lemma 4.117, Lemma 4.120, Definition 4.124, Definition 4.126, Lemma 4.127, and Definition 4.128 generate 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=2⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=2}[\mathsf{I}] in dependency order. Proposition 4.129 gives the cocycle condition for the local order-coordinate chart-change maps.

For k=3k=3, the implication chain of Proposition 4.145, Lemma 4.133, Definition 4.135, Lemma 4.136, Definition 4.142, and Lemma 4.139 generate 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋=3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{=3}[\mathsf{I}] in dependency order.

The recursive formula of Definition 4.144 gives each cumulative bundle. Definition 4.140 and Proposition 4.141 give the composition formula, equivalence-class judgment, and strict-order judgment for the representational coordinate. Proposition 4.143 gives the local-to-global compatibility identity with the local comparison chart. ∎

Assume

𝖮𝗉𝖾𝗋𝖺𝗍𝗂𝗏𝖾⁢(𝒳)\mathsf{Operative}(\mathcal{X})

𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤k⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq k}[\mathsf{I}]
