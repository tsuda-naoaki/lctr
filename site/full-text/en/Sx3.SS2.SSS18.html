Changes of a black hole or of a system of black holes select a preferred direction in time

The original-source location is Bekenstein, Black Holes and Entropy, Physical Review D 7, p. 2334, §II.

Verbatim Original (English):

purely formal nature

At the end of Section II on pp. 2334–2335, the source describes the analogy developed up to that stage as formal. Sections III and IV then provide a physical construction of black-hole entropy based on information theory.

Verbatim Original (English):

natural to conjecture

Page 2339, §V, presents the generalized second law as a conjecture. Page 2338, §IV, introduces TbhT_{\mathrm{bh}} in Eq. (18) as a characteristic temperature and cautions against identifying it with the actual black-hole temperature.
