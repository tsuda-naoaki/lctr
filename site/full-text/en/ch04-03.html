For each symbol root, one pair consisting of a role type and a reception phase first appears in complete form. 4.3.1 Operability Theorem

Comparison-stage operability is equivalent to the conjunction of the stage-seven satisfaction bundle and the identity-action condition for every pure transport loop.

For every realized operational configuration 𝒳\mathcal{X} and typed family of comparison rules 𝒥cmp\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{cmp}},

𝖮𝗉𝖾𝗋𝖺𝗍𝗂𝗏𝖾⁢(𝒳)\displaystyle\mathsf{Operative}(\mathcal{X})

Proof.

By Definition 4.78, 𝖫≤7⁢[𝖤𝗏cmp]\mathsf{L}_{\leq 7}\left[\mathsf{Ev}^{\mathrm{cmp}}\right] is the stage-seven satisfaction bundle.

By Definition 4.86, comparison-stage operability is equivalent to the conjunction of the stage-seven satisfaction bundle and the identity-action condition for every pure transport loop. The claim follows. ∎

4.3.2 Canonical Uniqueness and Universality

Under exact operability, the canonical objects of the comparison and representation stages are uniquely determined by the realized operational configuration and the typed family of comparison rules. Admissible comparison representations, admissible local order-atlas representations, and admissible global order representations factor, respectively, as compositions with the canonical projection, the local order-coordinate projections, and the canonical global order-coordinate projections.

Assume 𝖤𝗑𝖺𝖼𝗍𝖮𝗉𝖾𝗋𝖺𝖻𝗅𝖾⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ExactOperable}\left[\mathsf{I}\right]. Then

(𝖰𝗎can⁢[𝖨],𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨])\left(\mathsf{Qu}^{\mathrm{can}}[\mathsf{I}]\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak% \mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}]\right)

is uniquely determined by 𝖨\mathsf{I}.

Proof.

By Proposition 4.176, comparison-stage operability, the canonical-order descent condition, the local incomparability transitivity condition, and the global incomparability transitivity condition that constitute the right-hand side of its equivalence all hold.

By Theorem 4.90, the extended comparison-reachability equivalence relation, the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} comparison quotient set, the canonical projection, the quotient relation derived from ℭ⁢𝔇\mathfrak{C}\mathfrak{D}, and the canonical comparison quotient derived from ℭ⁢𝔇\mathfrak{C}\mathfrak{D} are uniquely determined in dependency order.

By the representative invariance in Definition 4.99, the right-hand side of Definition 4.101 is invariant under the choice of representative of a comparison equivalence class. Hence, the canonical ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} partial orders are uniquely determined as binary relations on the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} comparison quotient sets. By Definition 4.103,

ℰop[𝖨]=(𝖰𝗎can[𝖨],(⪯Rcan[𝖨])R∈𝖱𝗈𝗅cmp)\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{op}}[\mathsf{I}]=\left(\mathsf{Qu}^{\mathrm{can}}[\mathsf% {I}]\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak\left(\preceq_{R}^{\mathrm{can}}[\mathsf{I}]% \right)_{R\in\mathsf{Rol}^{\mathrm{cmp}}}\right)

is uniquely determined.

Definition 4.111 determines the local incomparability relations on each local canonical quotient domain from the canonical strict ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} orders. By Lemma 4.114, the local incomparability relations are equivalence relations. Definition 4.116 uniquely determines the local order-coordinate quotients and the local strict linear orders. Definition 4.119 uniquely determines the local order-coordinate projections, and Definition 4.124 uniquely determines the local comparison charts as compositions of the canonical projection with the local order-coordinate projections.

By Lemma 4.127, the local order-coordinate chart-transition maps of Definition 4.126 are representative-invariant and uniquely determined. By Definition 4.128, the family of local order-coordinate projections, local comparison charts, and local order-coordinate chart-transition maps uniquely determines the comparison-order atlas.

By Definition 4.142, each local-to-global order-coordinate embedding is uniquely determined as the map that sends a local incomparability class to the global incomparability class of the same representative. ∎
