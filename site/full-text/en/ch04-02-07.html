Proposition 4.169 (Component types of continuum-approximation evaluation input).

The componentwise establishment-judgment formulas and the types of the Boolean truth-value components of the continuum-approximation evaluation-input datum are

𝖤𝗏𝖺𝗅κcont⁢[𝖨]\displaystyle\mathsf{Eval}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\kappa}[\mathsf{I}] ⪯κcont,eval𝖳𝗈𝗅κcont,κ∈𝖤𝗏𝖺𝗅𝖨𝖽𝗑cont,\displaystyle\preceq_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{cont\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak eval}% }\mathsf{Tol}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\kappa}\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak\qquad% \kappa\in\mathsf{EvalIdx}^{\mathrm{cont}}\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak
(𝖢𝖾𝗅𝗅𝖶𝗂𝖽𝖠𝖽𝗆𝔇⁢[𝖨],𝖳𝗈𝗄𝖲𝖾𝗉ℭ⁢𝔇rec⁢[𝖨])\displaystyle\left(\mathsf{CellWidAdm}_{\mathfrak{D}}[\mathsf{I}]\mathchar 44% \relax\allowbreak\mathsf{TokSep}^{\mathrm{rec}}_{\mathfrak{C}\mathfrak{D}}[% \mathsf{I}]\right) ∈𝖡𝗈𝗈𝗅2\displaystyle\in\mathsf{Bool}^{2}

The candidate datum for a continuous coordinate representation derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} has as components a coordinate carrier, a linear order, an order topology, and a comparison representational coordinate.

Proof.

Definition 4.164 gives the six 𝖤𝗏𝖺𝗅𝖢𝗈𝗆𝗉κcont\mathsf{EvalComp}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\kappa} an evaluation-value set, a partial order, a least element, an evaluation value, and an admissible threshold. Definition 4.165 gives order-preserving representational stability as the κ=𝖮𝗋𝖽𝖲𝗍𝖺𝖻\kappa=\mathsf{OrdStab} component of the same component family. Definition 4.166 gives the two truth-value components, and Definition 4.167 gives the candidate for a continuous coordinate representation derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}.

Thus the tuple of Definition 4.168 has as components conditional quantitative affine realizations, finite operational granularity, six componentwise evaluation types, two Boolean truth values, and candidates for continuous coordinate representations derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}. The componentwise establishment judgment and continuous-representation evaluation apply the evaluation relation to the corresponding components, and the complete evaluation specification determines the establishment value of the continuum approximation. ∎
