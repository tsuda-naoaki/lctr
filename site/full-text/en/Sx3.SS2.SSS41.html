Definition S.402.

Placement of grounding responsibilities

Placement of grounding responsibilities

The seven time contents audited in this source are not generated from nontemporal physical inputs before use; they are accepted as already established temporal inputs. The placement judgment for the physical-time grounding responsibility is ”backgrounded by primitive time input.” This judgment is based on comparing, for each time content, its use in a law operation or result derivation against the absence, before that use, of a corresponding generation condition or condition of validity stated as an input, formation condition, evaluation condition, or failure condition.

Evidence of actual failure

Evidence of actual failure

The complete-generation judgment and the remaining-root judgment concern qualification as physical time and are distinct from actual failure of a concrete condition governing the value or domain of application of a result. Neither the source’s formal evidence nor the LCTR audit evidence establishes actual failure of such a target condition. The actual-failure judgment is ”indeterminate from the original source evidence”; actual failure is not inferred from the backgrounding of a grounding responsibility alone.

Downstream reach

Downstream reach

The scope of reach to results dependent on primitive time roots is determined source by source by the primitive-time-root/result dependency relation reconstructed in this division and by minimal primitive-time-root support. This is structural reach. Because no actual failure of a concrete condition has been established, the judgment of actual reach from a failure location to results is ”indeterminate from the original source evidence.” Structural reach does not substitute for actual reach.

Repair scope

Repair scope

On the basis of structural reach, the repair targets are the corresponding time contents and the generation conditions or conditions of validity required before their use. The recalculation scope is restricted to results reached by dependency from a failure location whose actual failure has been established independently. Because actual failure is indeterminate in this source, this repair and recalculation specification is conditional. If the condition is met, the target results are regenerated or recalculated from repaired inputs, or their domain of validity is restricted. Values after recalculation are determined as audit results.

Individual statements

Decoherence, collapse description, and experimental verification

The statement concerns the process described by decoherence, its relation to the former name ”collapse of the wave function,” and the scope of the object subjected to experimental verification. Original (English):

‘decoherence’ which describes the process that used to be called ‘collapse of the wave function’. The concept is now experimentally verified

The source location is Philip W. Anderson, Science: A “Dappled World” or a “Seamless Web”?, p. 492.

The minimal context is a discussion of the quantum measurement problem, decoherence, collapse processes, and verification by atomic-beam techniques. The quoted passage states in succession the object explained by decoherence, the former collapse designation, and experimental verification. The attributed author is Philip W. Anderson. The predicates assigned to the objects are that decoherence describes the process formerly called collapse, and that ”The concept” is now experimentally verified. The explicit domain of application comprises the explanation of the quantum measurement problem, decoherence, and collapse on p. 492, and the subsequent discussion of atomic-beam verification.

Individual audits

Decoherence, collapse description, and experimental verification

The original and its minimal context permit the following four interpretations.

  1. 1.

    An interpretation that treats decoherence and the process formerly called collapse as the same object of explanation

  2. 2.

    A restricted interpretation under which decoherence describes the observable process of interference suppression associated with collapse descriptions

  3. 3.

    An interpretation that attaches ”The concept” to the concept of decoherence and states its experimental verification

  4. 4.

    An interpretation that attaches ”The concept” to the entire immediately preceding process description and states the experimental verification of a broader process that includes collapse

The four possible interpretations coexist.

The independent basis is the explanatory relation between decoherence and the former collapse designation, and the existing cases of atomic-beam verification, which the source places in the same descriptive sequence. Anderson uses existing experiments within a historical and philosophical argument.

The boundaries of evidential force under the respective interpretations are the following five.

  1. 1.

    Formal equivalence between decoherence and collapse does not follow from the same-object interpretation.

  2. 2.

    The restricted interpretation is consistent with experimental support for interference suppression but does not imply the generation of a single outcome with probabilities.

  3. 3.

    The experimental force of the decoherence-attachment interpretation belongs to the decoherence phenomena and verification cases mentioned in the source.

  4. 4.

    Direct experimental resolution of collapse as a whole or of the quantum measurement problem as a whole does not follow from the broader-attachment interpretation.

  5. 5.

    The physical generation of the time contents used by decoherence, collapse, and atomic-beam processes does not follow from this source.
