Conditions and Domain of Application of Direct Measurement

The paper refers to an experimental-physics basis, physically identifies the vacuum speed of light, and cites Michelson’s experiment. These are not new measured values reported in this paper.

Objects of Formal Generalization

Results 1–10 apply to Newtonian mechanics, Lorentz transformations, the four-dimensional world, the light cone, and causal classification. Results 11–14 apply to proper time on timelike world-lines and covariant mechanics, and results 15–16 apply to the four-dimensional electromagnetic field. Conditions and Domain of Application of Formal Generalization

The Newtonian group expresses form invariance of the mechanical equations considered in the source. The Lorentz group and temporal-order preservation in results 9–10 concern c>0c>0 and transformations preserving temporal orientation. The Newtonian limit takes c→∞c\to\infty at fixed physical relative velocity. Results 11–14 obey the curve, differentiation, mass and coordinate conditions stated above. In result 14, subtracting m⁢c2mc^{2} and taking the low-speed limit yields Newtonian kinetic energy. Result 15 uses source charge, world-line, velocity and acceleration, together with receiver motion, on the domain where the retarded intersection exists. Evidence Effect

Evidence-Effect Domain

The formal evidence in this reference directly supports 17 results concerning the Newton and Lorentz groups, the four-dimensional world, the light cone, the directional classification of world-points, proper time, covariant mechanics, the four-dimensional electromagnetic field, and the limited proposed theory of gravitation. The possibility of sending and receiving light supports the physical interpretation of the past and future light cones.

Evidence-Effect Boundary

The establishment of the four-dimensionally covariant form and the division of world-points by the light cone does not imply that coordinate time, proper time, the light-speed ratio, or temporal order has been generated from non-temporal physical inputs. The unification of mathematical form, the reference to an empirical basis, and the possibility of sending and receiving light likewise do not retroactively confer physical-time qualification on the 32 time contents. These transitions lie beyond the evidence-effect boundary under Definition 7.115.

Result-Preservation Domain

Result-Preservation Domain

The 17 results are preserved insofar as each time-content prerequisite is supplied by the corresponding generated time representation in the LCTR generation sequence, while retaining the original-source inputs and dependency directions comprising world-points, world-lines, emission, propagation, reception, the quadratic form, transformations preserving temporal orientation, bodies, constant mass, force, charge, current, and electromagnetic fields. The light cone and causal classification are preserved as relations among world-points and the possibility of sending and receiving light signals on generated time representations. Proper time and its derivatives obey the specified curve, segment and requisite differentiability. Retarded fields apply where the retarded intersection exists. The domain of application of the limited proposed theory of gravitation is determined by the zero source acceleration, additional factor, and low-speed approximation required in the original source.

Placement of Grounding Obligations

Placement of Grounding Obligations

The source places world-lines in a four-dimensional world supplied with coordinate time and temporal orientation, and defines proper time by integrating the world-line element. It specifies conditions on timelikeness, differentiability, transformation groups and mechanics, but uses the 32 time contents without generating them from non-temporal physical inputs. The LCTR classification of the placement of the physical-time grounding obligation is therefore “backgrounded by primitive-time inputs.”

Evidence of Actual Invalidation

Evidence of Actual Invalidation

The complete judgment and remaining-root judgment concern physical-time qualification and are distinct from the actual invalidation of a specific condition that determines a result’s value or domain of application. The original-source formal evidence and LCTR audit evidence for this reference do not establish the actual invalidation of such a target condition. The actual-invalidation judgment is “indeterminate from original-source evidence”; actual invalidation is not inferred from the backgrounding of the grounding obligation alone.

Downstream Reach

Downstream Reach

The reach to primitive-time-root-dependent results is determined for this reference by the primitive-time-root–result dependence relations and minimal primitive-time-root supports reconstructed in this division. This is structural reach. Because the actual invalidation of a specific condition has not been established, the judgment of actual reach from the invalidation position to a result is “indeterminate from original-source evidence.” Structural reach does not substitute for actual reach.

Repair Scope

Repair Scope

The repair targets based on structural reach are the corresponding time content and the generation or establishment conditions required before its use. The recalculation scope is limited to results for which actual invalidation is independently established and which are dependency-reachable from that invalidation position. Because actual invalidation is indeterminate in this reference, this repair and recalculation specification is conditional. If the condition is met, the target results are regenerated or recalculated from the repaired inputs, or their establishment domain is restricted.

Common Evidence with Domain of Application

Individual Audit

Interpretive Possibilities

The first statement permits both a mathematical claim that unifies spatial and temporal coordinates within four-dimensional geometry and an ontological claim that rejects the separate independence of space and time and attributes independence to their combination. The second statement gives a mathematical convention defining the totality of quadruples of coordinate values as the world. The third statement gives a methodological proposal to express physical laws as interrelations among world-lines. The fourth statement gives a physical identification associating the vacuum propagation speed of light with the constant cc of the transformation group. The fifth statement permits both a geometrical interpretation allowing multiple time axes and spatial projections for one four-dimensional world and an ontological interpretation attributing independence to the world. The sixth statement gives an operational and geometrical relation that distinguishes the past and future light cones by the possibility of sending and receiving light signals.

Independent Grounds

The first and fifth statements present Minkowski’s view of space and time; the second defines the world, and the third proposes a world-line-based method. The fourth identifies the transformation-group constant with vacuum light speed; the sixth associates the two light-cone components with the sending and receiving of light signals.

Evidence-Effect Boundary

The first statement directly supports the change in attribution from the separate independence of space and time to their four-dimensional combination; the second directly supports the definition of the world as the totality of world-points; and the third directly supports the proposal to express physical laws as relations among world-lines. The fourth statement directly supports the correspondence between cc and the vacuum propagation speed of light, but does not support the physical generation of the time content contained in the speed representation. The fifth statement directly supports the relation between multiple spatial projections and one four-dimensional world. The scope of the sixth statement is the correspondence between the two light-cone components and the possibility of sending and receiving light. None of the six statements directly supports either the generation, solely from non-temporal physical inputs, of coordinate tt, the temporal component of the speed of light, world-line order, light-cone order, or proper time, or their physical-time qualification.

Corresponding Formal Evidence

The first statement corresponds to claims 1–4 and result 1; the second, to time content 4, claims 13–14 and result 4; the third, to time contents 5–8, claims 15–20 and results 5–6; the fourth, to time content 11, claims 23–28 and result 3; the fifth, to time contents 12–13 and 15, claims 29–37 and results 7–8; and the sixth, to time content 16, claims 38–39 and result 9. Time-content, claim and result indices are specified by Definitions S.26, S.27 and S.29, respectively.

Common Audit with Domain of Application

Within-Reference Comparison

The first, second, and fifth statements integrate space and time into a four-dimensional world; the third places physical laws as relations among world-lines; and the fourth and sixth connect light propagation to the transformation constant and the distinction between light-cone components. These statements constitute a single geometrical system, but that system does not eliminate the separate obligation to generate physically the time coordinate and order used within it.
