Proposition 4.157 (Structural separation of finite operational granularity input).

Under 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖢𝗍𝗑≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprCtx}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}], Theorem 4.147 generates 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}] in dependency order. Once the selected representational codomain derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} and the order embedding into the representational codomain derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} are given, the comparison representational coordinate derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} is also determined.

The finite operational granularity datum has the five-component product type

𝖦𝗋𝗇op⁢(𝒳)∈𝖦𝗋𝗇𝖵𝖺𝗅op\mathsf{Grn}^{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal{X})\in\mathsf{GrnVal}^{\mathrm{op}}

and supplies inputs for evaluating the counting-step width derived from ℭ\mathfrak{C}, judging the admissibility of the record-cell width derived from 𝔇\mathfrak{D}, and evaluating the continuum approximation.

Proof.

Theorem 4.147 generates the components of 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}], and Definition 4.140 generates the comparison representational coordinate derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}. Definition 4.148 equips the selected representational codomain derived from ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} with a quantitative affine structure, and Definition 4.156 makes the five components a product-type datum on 𝖦𝗋𝗇𝖵𝖺𝗅op\mathsf{GrnVal}^{\mathrm{op}}. These results establish the generation dependencies of the representation-stage structure, the type of the finite operational granularity datum, and the three constructions that use that datum. ∎
