Assumptions

Assumption identifier

Assumption content

Davis and Lineweaver use cosmic proper time, the scale factor, radial null propagation and distance conventions in homogeneous, isotropic Robertson–Walker models to discuss recession velocities, horizons, reception and supernova observations. Each time content is evaluated for a selected model, source and observation epoch. The particle horizon is determined by integration from a selected initial endpoint, and the event horizon by integration to a selected future endpoint. An infinite future and a finite collapse endpoint belong to different models. Each supernova record belongs to its own observational clock process, calibration and selection conditions.

Generation Judgment

The complete LCTR generation count and source-evidence-conditioned local-reach count are both zero, leaving 26 primitive-time roots. Cosmic proper time, expansion history, light-propagation endpoints, clock widths, observation epochs and future comparison baselines supply temporal inputs to their respective relations.

Result Dependence

Of the 29 results, 28 depend on primitive-time roots. Result 15 is reached from source formal evidence for the algebraic Doppler relation. There are 80 root–result dependence pairs, of which 42 are necessary.

Placement of the Grounding Obligation

Davis and Lineweaver supply cosmological time, expansion histories, endpoints and observational time widths to model calculations and observational comparisons. These temporal uses carry the physical-time grounding obligation; the LCTR placement judgment is “backgrounded by primitive-time inputs.”

Evidence of Actual Failure

Each result’s domain is determined by the selected cosmological model, expansion history, distance convention, clock and light-propagation procedures, and observational conditions. Actual failure of a specific physical-time generation condition and the resulting change in predictions are undetermined by the source evidence.

Downstream Reach

Structural reach is determined by the 80 root–result dependence pairs. Actual reach from failure of a specific generation condition to a result is undetermined by the source evidence.

Repair Scope

The repair targets are each temporal use and the physical-time generation conditions required before it. If actual failure of a specific condition is established, recalculation concerns the results dependent on it.
