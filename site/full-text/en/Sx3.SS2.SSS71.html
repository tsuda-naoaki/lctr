Audit Conclusions

Proposition S.695 (Time-Use Audit of the Explicit Finite Decomposition of the Narlikar Source).

Both complete generation and local reach yield zero cases, and all fourteen time contents remain as primitive time roots. Each of the ten results depends on at least one of them.

Proof.

No time content is supplied at the generation starting point. Each of the eight listed transfers inputs at least one time content; none has only nontemporal physical inputs. Thus the generation closure adds no time content, and all fourteen contents remain primitive time roots. Every one of the ten result-input sets is nonempty. This determination concerns the source mapping and finite decomposition above; the calculation of finite proper time depends on temporal variables in the model. ∎

Common Evidence with a Specified Domain of Application

Individual Audit

Transfer from Signal Relations to an Attribute of Temporal Flow

Interpretive possibility

In the context of signal exchange between A and B, the original sentence makes time the subject that flows and its rate the attribute that differs.

Independent grounds

The corrected form of Eq. (13.30), consistent with the radial null condition in Eq. (13.22); the correspondence between boundary proper time and external coordinate time in Eq. (13.26); finite proper time in Example 13.2.2; and the qualitative account of gravitational and Doppler redshift.

Boundary of evidential force

The derivations and diagram concern relations among proper time tt, coordinate time TT, boundary motion, and light signals. The statement explains these relations by attributing different rates of flow to time for the two subjects.

Corresponding formal evidence

This audit corresponds to Time Contents 1–14 in Definition S.690, Definition S.692, and Results 1–10.
