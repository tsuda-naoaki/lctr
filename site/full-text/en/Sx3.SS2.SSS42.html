Evidential force

The formal evidence supports exponential decay, the Lorentzian line shape, inverse proportionality between uncertainty and lifetime, model deviations, and the Fano line shape. Its theoretical validity does not imply that Planck’s constant, angular frequencies, rate constants, lifetimes, or other time contents have been generated from nontemporal inputs.

Result-preservation domain

The five results are preserved where their time-content premises are generated by the LCTR generation sequence and the conditions in Table 385 are satisfied.

Placement of grounding responsibilities

Placement of grounding responsibilities

The eight time contents audited in this source are not generated from nontemporal physical inputs before use; they are accepted as already established temporal inputs. The definition of the second counts an existing oscillation period; the realization conditions and quantitative models in Claims 21 and 22 are not conditions that generate that period from nontemporal inputs. The placement judgment for the physical-time grounding responsibility is ”backgrounded by primitive time input.” This judgment is based on comparing, for each time content, its use in a law operation, unit definition, or result derivation against the absence, before that use, of a corresponding generation condition or condition of validity stated as an input, formation condition, evaluation condition, or failure condition.

Evidence of actual failure

Evidence of actual failure

The complete-generation judgment and the remaining-root judgment concern qualification as physical time and are distinct from actual failure of a concrete condition governing the value or domain of application of a result. Neither the source’s formal evidence nor the LCTR audit evidence establishes actual failure of such a target condition. The actual-failure judgment is ”indeterminate from the original source evidence”; actual failure is not inferred from the backgrounding of a grounding responsibility alone.

Downstream reach

Downstream reach

The scope of reach to results dependent on primitive time roots is determined source by source by the primitive-time-root/result dependency relation reconstructed in this division and by minimal primitive-time-root support. This is structural reach. Because no actual failure of a concrete condition has been established, the judgment of actual reach from a failure location to results is ”indeterminate from the original source evidence.” Structural reach does not substitute for actual reach.

Repair scope

Repair scope

On the basis of structural reach, the repair targets are the corresponding time contents and the generation conditions or conditions of validity required before their use. The recalculation scope is restricted to results reached by dependency from a failure location whose actual failure has been established independently. Because actual failure is indeterminate in this source, this repair and recalculation specification is conditional. If the condition is met, the target results are regenerated or recalculated from repaired inputs, or their domain of validity is restricted. Values after recalculation are determined as audit results.

Common evidence with specified domain of application

Individual audits

Rate constant and state lifetime

The source uses Γ\Gamma in Equation (1) as the rate constant of exponential decay and identifies it with the inverse lifetime of the high-energy state. The independent basis is a theoretical model that represents the radiation intensity after state preparation by an exponential-decay equation and relates its rate constant to the lifetime. The evidential force of this relation belongs to the exponential-decay relation under the simplifying assumptions imposed by the source. The corresponding formal evidence consists of Source Claims 9–11 in Table 381, Time Contents 3, 4, and 6 in Table 383, Transfer 2 in Table 384, Result 1 in Table 385, and Proposition S.409.

Width of the spectral feature

In Equation (2), Γ\Gamma is the angular-frequency full width at half maximum of the Lorentzian line shape. In Equation (3), Γ\Gamma is a detuning scale and ϵ=2⁢(ω−ω0)/Γ\epsilon=2(\omega-\omega_{0})/\Gamma is dimensionless. The full width at half maximum of a general Fano line shape is not identified with Γ\Gamma. The independent basis is the Lorentzian line shape representing exponential decay in the angular-frequency domain and the role of Γ\Gamma within that line shape. The evidential force of this relation belongs to the line shape in Equation (2). The corresponding formal evidence consists of Source Claims 12–15 in Table 381, Time Contents 2, 4, and 5 in Table 383, Result 2 in Table 385, and Proposition S.409.

Energy uncertainty and lifetime

The source states that energy uncertainty is inversely proportional to lifetime. Result 3 specifies this uncertainty as the Lorentzian full width at half maximum in Equation (2), Δ⁢EFWHM=ℏ⁢Γ=ℏ/τ\Delta E_{\mathrm{FWHM}}=\hbar\Gamma=\hbar/\tau. This width is not the standard deviation of energy. The independent basis is a theoretical relation that uses Γ\Gamma both as the inverse of the lifetime and as the angular-frequency width and relates energy uncertainty to lifetime through Planck’s constant. The evidential force of this relation belongs to the inverse proportionality described by the source. The corresponding formal evidence consists of Source Claims 11, 12, 15, and 16 in Table 381, Time Contents 1, 4, and 6 in Table 383, Transfer 2 in Table 384, Result 3 in Table 385, and Proposition S.409.

Deviation from the Lorentzian form

The source states that the Lorentzian form rests on simplifying assumptions and that additional physical processes can produce significant deviations. The independent basis is a theoretical comparison between the simplified Lorentzian model and line shapes that include additional physical processes, alternative transition paths, and interference. The evidential force of this relation belongs to the additional physical processes and interference conditions described by the source. The corresponding formal evidence consists of Source Claims 14, 17, and 18 in Table 381, Time Contents 2, 4, and 5 in Table 383, Results 4 and 5 in Table 385, and Proposition S.409.

Common audit with specified domain of application

The four statements concern, respectively, decay rate and lifetime, Lorentzian width, energy width and lifetime, and possible deviations due to additional physical processes. The individual audits above specify the conditions of application and corresponding formal evidence for each relation.

The four statements do not provide the physical generation of the time contents carried by the rate constant, angular-frequency width, state lifetime, and spectral angular frequency.

The corresponding formal evidence is given in Table 383, Table 381, Table 384, Table 385, Definition S.407, and Proposition S.409. Time contents reached from nontemporal inputs are 0/8, primitive time roots are 8/8, and root-dependent results are 5/5.
