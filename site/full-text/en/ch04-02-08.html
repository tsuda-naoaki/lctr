4.2.8 Consequences of the Representation Stage

Proposition 4.170 (Separation of representation-stage structures from quantitative-evaluation inputs).

Assume 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖢𝗍𝗑≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprCtx}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}]. For each R∈𝖱𝗈𝗅cmpR\in\mathsf{Rol}^{\mathrm{cmp}}, specify the selected ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived representational value range and its ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived representational-value-range order embedding from Definition 4.140. Then

𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\displaystyle\mathsf{Repr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}] ⟹𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨],\displaystyle\Longrightarrow\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}]\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak
𝖱𝖾𝗉Rcmp⁢[𝖨]\displaystyle\mathsf{Rep}^{\mathrm{cmp}}_{R}[\mathsf{I}] =𝖱𝖾𝗉𝖤𝗆𝖻Rord⁢[𝖨]∘𝖯𝗋𝗃Rord,can⁢[𝖨]\displaystyle=\mathsf{RepEmb}^{\mathrm{ord}}_{R}[\mathsf{I}]\circ\mathsf{Prj}_% {R}^{\mathrm{ord\mathchar 44\relax\allowbreak can}}[\mathsf{I}]

are determined in dependency order. The components of 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}] are given in Definition 4.144.

Further specify the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived conditional quantitative affine realizations of Definition 4.148, the finite operational granularity datum of Definition 4.156, and the six componentwise evaluation inputs of Definition 4.164. Under these specifications, the 𝔇\mathfrak{D}-derived record-cell-width admissibility predicate, the ℭ\mathfrak{C}-token record-separation predicate, and the order-topological coordinate candidates are determined and form the continuum-approximation evaluation-input datum of Definition 4.168. The bundles of structures generated at the representation stage and the quantitative-evaluation inputs have distinct types and dependency orders.

Proof.

Theorem 4.147 generates 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋𝖲𝗍𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{ReprStr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}], and Definition 4.140 gives the factorization of the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived comparison representational coordinates. The specified quantitative affine realizations satisfy the compatibility conditions of Definition 4.148. Definition 4.156 forms a product-type datum from the five specified granularity components. Definitions 4.160 and 4.162 determine record-cell-width admissibility and record separation from the generated record images and the specified inputs. Definition 4.167 determines the order-topological coordinate candidates from the order on the selected representational value ranges and the comparison representational coordinates. Definition 4.168 collects these objects, together with the six specified componentwise evaluation inputs, into one tuple. Hence, the bundles of structures generated at the representation stage, the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived comparison representational coordinates, and the quantitative-evaluation inputs satisfy the types and dependency orders stated in the proposition. ∎

The object 𝖱𝖾𝗉𝗋≤3⁢[𝖨]\mathsf{Repr}_{\leq 3}[\mathsf{I}] of Definition 4.92 expresses the conjunction of the three representation-stage conditions. Specifying the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived conditional quantitative affine realizations determines quantitative affine differences and frequency and period indexed by admissible counting windows. Source-relative frequency requires window invariance on a nonempty family of windows; a hertz numerical value requires a positive unit scalar. Specifying a unit through a standard frequency uses a reference source and a normalization convention. Further specifying finite operational granularity and the six componentwise evaluation inputs determines the continuum-approximation evaluation-input datum, together with record-cell-width admissibility, record separation, and coordinate candidates. Proposition 4.172 expresses tuple construction when these direct inputs are given.

The structures generated at each stage of Table 2 are as follows.

Comparison-input layer

The ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-source-induced arrival preorders are determined from the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} source-side partial orders, the comparison-side recovery maps, and the tagged comparison-arrival reception spaces.

Canonical-order layer

When KCanOrdDescrepr⁢[𝖨]K^{\mathrm{repr}}_{\mathrm{CanOrdDesc}}[\mathsf{I}] holds, the source-induced arrival preorders descend to the comparison equivalence classes and determine the canonical ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} partial orders and the exact operational time structures.

Local order-coordinate layer

When KLocIncTransrepr⁢[𝖨]K^{\mathrm{repr}}_{\mathrm{LocIncTrans}}[\mathsf{I}] holds, it determines the quotients by local incomparability classes, the local strict linear orders, the local order-coordinate projections, and the local comparison charts. The local order-coordinate chart-transition maps yield the comparison-order atlas.

Global order-coordinate layer

When KGlobIncTransrepr⁢[𝖨]K^{\mathrm{repr}}_{\mathrm{GlobIncTrans}}[\mathsf{I}] holds, it determines the quotients by global incomparability classes, the global strict linear orders, and the canonical global order-coordinate projections. Their composition with the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived representational-value-range order embeddings yields the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived comparison representational coordinates.

Quantitative-representation layer

Specifying the ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D}-derived conditional quantitative affine realizations determines quantitative affine differences and frequency and period indexed by admissible counting windows. Source-relative frequency and hertz numerical values use the window-family, window-invariance, and unit-normalization inputs specified in their respective definitions.

Continuum-approximation evaluation-input layer

Further specify the finite operational granularity datum and the six componentwise evaluation inputs. Each evaluation input includes an evaluation-value carrier, a partial order, a least element, an evaluation value, and an admissible threshold. Construct record-cell-width admissibility, record separation, and the order-topological coordinate candidates, and collect them into the tuple of Definition 4.168.

The ℭ/𝔇\mathfrak{C}/\mathfrak{D} source-side partial orders, the comparison-side recovery maps, and the tagged comparison-arrival reception spaces are the generative inputs to (⪯Rarr)R∈𝖱𝗈𝗅cmp(\preceq_{R}^{\mathrm{arr}})_{R\in\mathsf{Rol}^{\mathrm{cmp}}}.
