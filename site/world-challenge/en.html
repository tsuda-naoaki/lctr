An Indictment of Physics, a Challenge to the World

Physics has been engaging in serious misconduct for more than 2,400 years, ever since the natural philosophy of ancient Greece.

Architect “I will teach you how to build a ten-thousand-story building.

First, we have a foundation, of course.

Now let us design the columns and beams. We will calculate the strength of each floor with great precision. Calculation and experiment agree to a relative precision of 0.0000000023!※ We can build even higher!”

Student “Wait a moment. Have you investigated the ground?”

Architect “No.”

Student “Have you built the foundation?”

Architect “No.”

Student “Then where is this foundation?”

Architect “I don't know. Perhaps it has been there since the world began. I don't know who built it or how, and no one has ever checked the actual foundation.

But we take it for granted that the foundation exists, so we're building the ground floor on top of it. We calculate everything from the ground floor up with great precision.”

Student “What? Doesn't that frighten you?”

Architect “Why? The calculations are correct.”

Student “But what if things on site differ from the calculations?”

Architect “That would be wind, temperature, variations in the materials, or poor construction accuracy. It is not a problem with the plans.”

Student “What do you do about the discrepancies?”

Architect “That is the construction team's job, not the architect's responsibility. We have the people on site deal with it.”

The architect goes on designing ten thousand stories, then twenty thousand, without investigating the ground or building a foundation. However many calculations are performed for the upper floors, nobody has started building the foundation that must support their weight. But the calculations succeed with extraordinary precision, and problems on site are not the architects' fault, so they have no need to worry about the foundation.

Physics is doing exactly this.

Let us look at another example.

“I will teach you how to make a delicious cake. First, get a delicious sponge cake. I do not know who made it. I do not know how to make it, either. It has always been there. Anyone can use it without conditions. Now let us decorate it with cream.”

Nobody explains how to make a delicious sponge cake from flour and eggs.

Physics is doing exactly this.

What about theories that claim to have created time?

“I have discovered how to make a sponge cake from scratch.

First, get a strawberry-and-cream cake.
Carefully remove the strawberries and cream.
Wash what remains thoroughly.
Dry it thoroughly.

Look! I have made a sponge cake!”

The pastry instructor explains every step with a straight face.

The ingredient is called a “strawberry-and-cream cake”. It already contains a sponge cake that someone has baked. The instructor takes out the finished sponge cake, washes it, dries it, and claims to have “made it from scratch”.

Surely nobody would make such a ridiculous claim?

Physics really does this.

LCTR identifies this structure in earlier theories of time generation. The starting components have names other than “time”. Yet they already contain the time that the theory is supposed to create.

  • DeWitt (1967): the angular frequency of a model clock. DeWitt defines the model clock's time from its phase and angular frequency. Angular frequency expresses how far the phase advances per unit time. The clock construction also uses a rate of change with respect to the clock's own time. The inputs to the calculation that yields time already include a quantity per unit time and a rate of change with respect to time. Supplement S.2.14
  • Barbour (1994 I): the realized history of configurations. Barbour defines time differences using the relative configurations of the objects making up the universe. The integral uses the history of which configurations the objects actually moved from and to, together with the direction of that history. The calculation yielding a duration already depends on temporal content: the succession of the objects' configurations. Supplement S.2.33
  • Connes–Rovelli (1994): the correspondence between thermal time and physical time scales. Connes and Rovelli construct a mathematical flow called thermal time from a specified state and algebra. Their concrete examples connecting that flow to physical time scales compare scales using established time. LCTR identifies the problem at this point: the correspondence with a physical time scale depends on time that has already been supplied. Supplement S.2.35
  • Kiefer (2009): the Hubble constant used in numerical evaluation. Kiefer discusses an equation without external time and the time of a subsystem obtained through approximation. However, the numerical calculation evaluating how strongly quantum interference is suppressed uses the Hubble constant. This constant expresses the fractional expansion of the universe per unit time. Even after external time has been removed from the equation, this numerical evaluation takes a quantity involving time as an input. Supplement S.2.54

LCTR identifies the same circularity in all earlier theories of time generation. They are cheating by implicitly using time in the components from which they claim to create time. This is the second accusation.

Can time be defined in the first place?

In The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Feynman wrote:

“time is one of the things we probably cannot define (in the dictionary sense)”

He then turned from how to define time to how to measure it. Volume I, Chapter 5, Section 2

Professor Feynman, time can be defined. It can be constructed. I have actually constructed it.

LCTR identifies the minimum requirements for establishing time, specifies the role of each, and provides the procedure for constructing time all the way to completion.

In the cake analogy, it identifies the minimum ingredients needed for a sponge cake, explains the role of each, and shows how to bake the cake using only ingredients that have not yet become sponge cake.

LCTR claims to be the first theory in human history to achieve this. Its construction and verification materials are publicly available.

If an earlier theory has succeeded in doing this, please tell me about it.
If you find time already present in LCTR's components, please point out where.
If you find any other error in the construction, please point that out as well.
