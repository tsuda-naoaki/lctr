The eight PDFs are distributed separately, not included in this archive.
Place any required PDF in this directory with its published filename unchanged.
The mathematical verification sources and results form one shared set.

  lctr_main_supp_full_jp.pdf     Japanese full edition
  lctr_main_supp_full_en.pdf     English full edition
  lctr_main_jp.pdf               Japanese main text
  lctr_main_en.pdf               English main text
  lctr_supp_jp.pdf               Japanese supplement
  lctr_supp_en.pdf               English supplement
  lctr_figures_jp.pdf            Japanese figures and captions
  lctr_figures_en.pdf            English figures and captions

The primary correspondence links use the full editions. Main-only alternatives
refer to the same named destinations. Printed page and equation numbers are
inherited from the full edition. The back-matter pages are shared by the
main-only and supplement-only editions.

PDF-SHA256SUMS at the extracted root fixes the separate PDF identities. From
that root, verify downloaded files with sha256sum -c PDF-SHA256SUMS (or shasum
-a 256 -c PDF-SHA256SUMS). Missing editions can be downloaded when needed;
they are not required for native proof checking.
